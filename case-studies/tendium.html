Product Internship

Tendium: Proposal to Grow the Platform Internationally

Tendium is an AI-powered bid coordination and management platform that helps teams discover, track, and coordinate on tenders. As part of a hands-on product internship, I co-authored a case study proposing how Tendium could expand its footprint beyond its home market.

Company

Tendium

Role

Product Internship (with Emmitt)

Focus

International growth strategy

Deliverable

Go-to-market proposal

Overview

Tendium's platform uses AI to help teams coordinate bids — surfacing relevant tenders, tracking deadlines, and keeping stakeholders aligned throughout the bidding process. This case study asked a focused strategic question: how should Tendium prioritize and approach growing its platform internationally?

Approach

  • Evaluated Tendium's current product and market position to identify what made it defensible domestically.
  • Researched candidate international markets for tender/procurement activity, regulatory complexity, and competitive presence.
  • Assessed where Tendium's existing AI-driven workflow would need to adapt for new markets vs. where it could scale as-is.
  • Packaged findings into a phased proposal for international growth, built and presented alongside a fellow intern.

Why This Mattered

Growth proposals like this force a product thinker to reason beyond a single market's assumptions — pricing, regulation, competitive landscape, and user behavior can all shift internationally. This project was a chance to practice that kind of market-agnostic strategic thinking early, working directly with the Tendium team.
